# 2026 June 11 Virtual MEI Board Meeting

Present: Anna Kijas, Anna Plaksin, Martha Thomae Elias, Maristella Feustle, Benni Bohl, Stefan Münnich, David Weigl, Kristina Richts-Matthei, Laurent Pugin

Excused: Jessica Grimmer

Minutes: Anna Kijas

## Agenda
Welcome to Kristina Richts-Matthaei. She joins the MEI Board as the representative from the Mainz Academy

Discussion
- Tokyo MEC 2026 recap
    - 5 student bursaries were awarded
    - Approx. 90 people attended in person and around 30 attended online
    - David Lewis is putting the conference proceedings together

- Discussion from MEC community meeting
    - Community is still interested in identifying a communication platform that is not Slack
    - MEI Board will identify requirements and scope for a communication platform to begin the process

- MEC 2027 will be held in Rome during June 7-10 2027
    - Johannes Kepper has been appointed the Program Chair

- Sponsorships
    - MEI Board has put in-kind sponsorships on hold at this time and will only accept monetary sponsorships. There is currently no shared understanding regarding in-kind sponsorships and further discussion is required before we accept in-kind sponsorships.

- Calendering
    - Next Board meetings will be on July 27: 10 AM EDT / 4 PM CEST

**MEI Community**: If you have questions or agenda items you would like the MEI Board to consider in a future meeting, please reach out to Anna Kijas (anna.kijas at tufts.edu) with your input.
