### Music Encoding Conference 2027 in Rome, Italy
MEC 2027 will take place 7 June - 10 June 2027 at the [German Historical Institue](https://dhi-roma.it/index.php?id=home&L=24) (Deutsches Historisches Institut) in Rome, Italy. The Local Organizing Committee Chair is Dr. Vera Grund and Program Committee Chair is Johannes Kepper (Universität Paderborn, Germany).

Additional details will be posted on the conference web pages in the next few months.
