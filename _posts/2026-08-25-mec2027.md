### Music Encoding Conference 2027 in Rome, Italy
MEC 2027 will take place 7 June - 10 June 2027 at the [German Historical Institue](https://dhi-roma.it/index.php?id=home&L=24) (Deutsches Historisches Institut) in Rome, Italy. In support of the conference, the [Pontificio Istituto di Musica Sacra](https://www.musicasacra.va/en.html) is acting as a partner and an additional venue. 
The Local Organizing Committee Chair is PD Dr. Vera Grund and Program Committee Chair is Prof. Dr. Johannes Kepper (Universität Paderborn, Germany). 

Additional details will be posted on the conference web pages in the next few months.
